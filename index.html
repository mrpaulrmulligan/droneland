Recommendations

    Assumes a nadir camera with mechanical shutter (the Mavic 4 Pro and Mini 3 presets are electronic-shutter only and are flagged), focus set on the ground, constant flight speed and square pixels. The sharpness score combines lens sharpness and motion blur. Lens sharpness balances edge softness (easing as you stop down from wide open), diffraction (growing as you stop down, sooner on small pixels) and depth of field over the height variation, so each camera has a best aperture; motion blur comes from flight speed, shutter speed and GSD. Both are estimated as blur size in pixels at the edge of the frame. Lens sharpness is a general model, not a test of your specific lens. Exposure uses typical scene brightness for each light condition (clear midday sun = EV 16, bright sun = EV 15 at ISO 100) and assumes a correctly metered, average ground scene; very dark or bright ground (water, snow, fresh asphalt) needs adjusting. Stopping down lets in less light, so raise ISO rather than slow the shutter. Coverage time excludes takeoff and landing and adds 20% for turns. The altitude warning uses the common 120 m / 400 ft AGL limit; check your local rules and waivers.
